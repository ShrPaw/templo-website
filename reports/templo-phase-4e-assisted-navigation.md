# TEMPLO — Phase 4E Assisted Navigation & Beginner-Friendly UX

## 1. Problem
Some visitors — especially beginners, older adults, and non-technical users — may not know where to click, how to start, or what happens after contacting TEMPLO. The site was well-structured but lacked explicit guided navigation for first-time visitors.

## 2. Strategy
Guide users with a clear visual path: Impact → What can I do? → Help me choose → Show me prices → Ask on WhatsApp → Location. Add explicit "start here" guidance, improve CTA clarity, add trust blocks, and ensure older adults can navigate confidently.

## 3. What Changed

### New: "Empieza Aquí" Guided Section
- 4 numbered geometric steps after the Guía TEMPLO section
- Step 1: Ver experiencias → `/experiencias/`
- Step 2: Usar orientador → `#pathfinder`
- Step 3: Ver planes → `#plans`
- Step 4: Preguntar por WhatsApp → WhatsApp link
- Responsive: 4 cols desktop, 2x2 tablet, 1 col mobile
- Premium dark/gold geometric design with numbered circles

### New: "Qué pasa después de escribirnos" Trust Block
- 4 short steps explaining the WhatsApp process
- Placed between Plans and Method sections
- Compact, premium design
- CTA: "Escribir por WhatsApp"

### New: WhatsApp Floating Label
- Desktop: shows "¿No sabes qué elegir? Te orientamos" near the floating button
- Hidden on mobile to avoid blocking content
- Applied to all 4 pages

## 4. Older Adult / Beginner UX
- **"Empieza Aquí" section**: Explicit numbered path for first-time visitors
- **"Qué pasa después" block**: Removes uncertainty about what happens after WhatsApp contact
- **Clear CTA labels**: No vague "Conocer más" — every button says exactly what it does
- **WhatsApp floating label**: Contextual help near the most important CTA
- **Button sizes**: Already 44px+ height, maintained
- **Text contrast**: Strong white-on-dark, maintained
- **No hover-only actions**: All actions are visible and tappable

## 5. CTA Clarity
Every primary CTA now describes the specific action:
- "Ver planes y precios" (not just "Ver planes")
- "Preguntar por WhatsApp" (not "Agendar Visita" which sounds formal)
- "Ver experiencia →" (not "Conocer más →")
- "Ver ubicación en Google Maps" (not just "Cómo llegar")
- "Consultar cómo llegar" (specific action)
