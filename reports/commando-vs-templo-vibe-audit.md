# TEMPLO vs Commando Studio — Visual Vibe Audit

**Date:** 2026-05-12  
**Auditor:** Senior Boutique Fitness Art Director + Frontend Auditor  
**Scope:** Full-page visual/vibe comparison (AUDIT ONLY — no code changes)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**TEMPLO** is a premium, disciplined, stoic fitness studio website with strong brand identity (black/gold/Buddha). It communicates authority, technique, and exclusivity. However, it currently feels more like an **editorial luxury brand** than a **living, breathing training studio**.

**Commando Studio** is a vibrant, energetic, community-driven fitness brand that feels alive with movement, photography, and social proof. It creates immediate desire to train.

**The gap:** TEMPLO tells you it's premium. Commando makes you *feel* the energy.

**Key finding:** TEMPLO's visual foundation is strong. The issue is not design quality — it's **visual density, photography rhythm, and motion energy**. The site needs more human moments, more training photography, and more visual rhythm to bridge the gap between "premium brand" and "active studio."

---

## 2. What Commando Does Better

### 2.1 Visual Rhythm & Pacing
Commando alternates between:
- Full-width hero images
- Text blocks
- Service cards with hover reveals
- Photo carousels
- Stats/metrics sections
- Coach grids

This creates a **rhythm** that keeps the eye moving. TEMPLO has longer text blocks and more editorial spacing, which creates a slower, more static reading experience.

### 2.2 Photography Density
Commando uses **real training photography everywhere**:
- Hero: video/slideshow of actual classes
- Services: each class has its own hero image
- Lifestyle: full-width photo carousel of studio moments
- Coaches: real portrait photos
- Community: stats with photo backgrounds

TEMPLO relies heavily on **abstract editorial panels** (dark gradients, numbers, lines) for service cards and lifestyle sections. This creates a premium feel but lacks human energy.

### 2.3 Motion & Animation
Commando has:
- Auto-playing hero slideshow
- Hover reveals on service cards (image zoom + text overlay)
- Smooth scroll animations
- Photo carousel with arrows
- Stats counting animation

TEMPLO has:
- Subtle fade-in on scroll
- Activity reel (newly added)
- Hover effects on cards
- Method pillar reveal

Commando's motion feels **active and continuous**. TEMPLO's motion feels **subtle and controlled**.

### 2.4 Service Presentation
Commando presents each class with:
- Full-width image background
- Bold class name
- Short description
- "Conoce Más" + "Reservar" dual CTAs
- Hover reveals image zoom

TEMPLO presents services with:
- Abstract panel (number + label)
- Text description
- Single "Conocer más" link

Commando's approach creates **immediate desire** to try the class. TEMPLO's approach is more **informational** than emotional.

### 2.5 Community/Social Proof
Commando shows:
- "+2.5M classes" counter
- "+190 coaches" counter
- "+45 studios" counter
- Photo carousel of community
- "Endorfinas, comunidad y estilo de vida" messaging

TEMPLO has:
- Lifestyle section with abstract panels
- Energy strip with placeholder photos
- No social proof metrics

Commando creates **FOMO and belonging**. TEMPLO creates **aspiration** but less urgency.

### 2.6 Conversion Flow
Commando:
- "Reservar" button in navbar (persistent)
- "Reservar" CTAs on every service card
- "Comprar" on hero slides
- Clear pricing page with direct booking
- Multiple conversion paths

TEMPLO:
- "Agendar Visita" in navbar
- WhatsApp-first approach
- "Consultar este plan" on pricing
- Single conversion channel (WhatsApp)

Commando's approach is **multi-path**. TEMPLO's is **WhatsApp-centric** (which is appropriate for Bolivia but could be supplemented).

## 4. Main Vibe Gap

### The Core Issue: TEMPLO is Premium but Static

TEMPLO feels like a **luxury brand brochure**. Commando feels like a **studio you want to visit right now**.

**Why this happens:**

1. **Abstract panels instead of photography**
   - Service cards use dark gradient panels with numbers
   - Lifestyle section uses abstract panels with labels
   - Energy strip uses placeholder photos
   - Coaches have no photos (initials only)

2. **Text-heavy sections**
   - Method section is 5 pillars of text
   - Objectives section is FAQ-style text blocks
   - Service detail section repeats information

3. **Limited motion**
   - No hero slideshow/video
   - No photo carousel
   - Subtle scroll animations only
   - Activity reel is good but limited

4. **No social proof**
   - No class count
   - No member testimonials
   - No transformation photos
   - No community metrics

5. **Passive CTAs**
   - "Conocer más" is informational
   - "Consultar este plan" is passive
   - No "Reservar ahora" urgency

### What TEMPLO Needs

TEMPLO doesn't need to become Commando. It needs to add **human moments** and **visual energy** while preserving its **stoic premium identity**.

The goal: **"A disciplined studio alive with focused energy"** — not "a chaotic gym with loud energy."

- No horizontal overflow

### TEMPLO Mobile Weaknesses
- Service cards feel dense on mobile

- No photo-forward sections

### Commando Mobile Strengths

### Commando Mobile Weaknesses

**Verdict:** TEMPLO's mobile foundation is solid. The main improvement needed is **more photography** and **clearer CTAs** on mobile.
