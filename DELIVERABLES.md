## 3. Visual Design Recommendations

### Color Palette
| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#0a0a0a` | Main bg |
| Surface | `#111111` | Cards, sections |
| Surface 2 | `#1a1a1a` | Featured cards |
| Border | `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)` | Subtle dividers |
| Text Primary | `#f5f5f5` | Headlines, body |
| Text Secondary | `#b0b0b0` | Descriptions |
| Text Muted | `#888888` | Labels, meta |
| Accent | `#c9a96e` | Gold — CTAs, highlights |
| Accent Light | `#dfc08a` | Hover states |
| WhatsApp | `#25D366` | WhatsApp button |

### Typography
- **Display / Headlines:** Playfair Display (serif) — weight 400–600
- **Body / UI:** Inter (sans-serif) — weight 300–600
- **Scale:** Responsive with `clamp()`, min ~1rem, max ~4rem for hero

### Design Principles
- **Dark dominance:** 95%+ of the page is dark. Light text. Gold accents.
- **Generous whitespace:** Section padding 80–140px. Card padding 32–48px.
- **Minimal borders:** Use `2px` gold accents sparingly. Use `rgba` borders for subtle separation.
- **No rounded corners:** `border-radius: 2px` max. Sharp = premium.
- **No gradients on surfaces:** Flat dark surfaces. Gradient only on hero overlay and CTA section.
- **Photography:** Large, real, unfiltered. Gym photos with natural lighting.
- **Animations:** Only fade-in on scroll. No bouncing, sliding, or parallax.
- **Mobile-first:** All grids collapse to single column. Nav becomes hamburger.

### What to AVOID
- ❌ Neon colors or gradients
- ❌ Rounded cards with shadows
- ❌ Carousel sliders
- ❌ Particle effects or 3D
- ❌ Stock photos of models
- ❌ Auto-playing video
- ❌ Complex multi-level navigation

## 4. Photos/Videos to Request from Lucas

### Critical (must have)
1. **Hero image** — Wide shot of the gym interior, ideally with someone training. Dark, moody, atmospheric. Aspect ratio 16:9 or wider. Min 1920px wide.
2. **Main training area** — Wide shot showing equipment, space, lighting. Aspect ratio 16:7.
3. **Weights/equipment area** — Close or mid-range shot of the equipment. Aspect ratio 4:3.
4. **Calisthenics area** — If applicable. Pull-up bars, rings, etc. Aspect ratio 4:3.
5. **Lucas portrait** — Professional but approachable. Training environment preferred. Aspect ratio 3:4.
6. **Community photos (6)** — Real people training. Groups, pairs, individuals. Candid, not posed. Square (1:1) format preferred for Instagram grid.

### Nice to have
7. **Detail shots** — Close-ups: hands on barbell, chalk, shoes on floor, water bottle. For visual texture.
8. **Before/after** — Only if clients have authorized. With written permission.
9. **Short video clip** — 10–15 seconds of real training. For potential hero background video in future iteration.

### Photo Guidelines
- Natural or dramatic lighting (not fluorescent/flat)
- No heavy filters or HDR
- Real people, real training
- Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- High resolution (min 1500px on short side)

## 6. Pricing Strategy Recommendation

**Recommendation: Do NOT publish prices publicly.**

Reasoning:
- Premium positioning requires consultation-based selling
- WhatsApp inquiry creates a personal touchpoint
- Allows price flexibility based on the prospect
- Creates curiosity and reduces price-shopping
- Standard practice for premium gyms (Equinox doesn't list prices)

Implementation:
- All plan cards show "[Consultar]" or "Desde [precio]" if Lucas prefers partial transparency
- Each plan CTA goes to WhatsApp with a contextual pre-filled message
- The FAQ addresses "how much?" by directing to WhatsApp

## 8. Conversion-Focused Design Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| Only 5 nav items | Reduces decision fatigue. Focus on conversion path. |
| Hero has 2 CTAs | Primary = conversion. Secondary = education. Both lead to action. |
| Plans section before founder | Commercial priority. People want to know "what does it cost" before "who runs it." |
| WhatsApp over forms | WhatsApp is the dominant communication channel in Bolivia. Lower friction than forms. |
| Dark theme | Premium perception. Matches gym/fitness aesthetic. Reduces visual noise. |
| Sharp corners (2px radius) | Signals precision, discipline, seriousness. Rounded = casual. |
| Gold accent color | Luxury association. Not red (aggressive), not blue (corporate). |
| FAQ as accordion | Reduces page length. Answers objections without overwhelming. |
| Floating WhatsApp button | Always accessible. Never more than one tap away from conversation. |
| Minimal animations | Respects user attention. Doesn't distract from the message. |
